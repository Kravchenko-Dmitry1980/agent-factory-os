# Operator Boundaries

Operators work **with** the lab — they do not productize it without charter.

---

## Allowed

- Run demos and evaluation scripts
- Read and improve operator-playbooks
- Write change proposals
- Local notes in evaluation/reports/
- Promote ideas through governance process

---

## Forbidden without review

- New runtime / orchestration engine
- CI/CD or dashboards
- Modifying Books/ or experiments/ as operator task
- Weakening gates for demo convenience
- Universal adapter/framework extraction

---

## Human-operated forever

- Final accept/reject on gated actions
- Promotion into agent-os
- Verdict on gate-touching merges
- Interpretation of "uncertain" traces
