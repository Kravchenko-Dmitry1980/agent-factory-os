# No Platform Drift

Operators and AI assistants must resist turning the lab into a platform.

---

## Stop phrases

- "Let's productize this"
- "One runner for all workflows"
- "Extract to shared engine"
- "Add CI so we don't forget tests"

---

## Correct responses

- Link prototype boundaries doc
- Run smoke manually
- Keep demos independent
- Document in operator-playbooks, don't automate

---

## Watch files

- Growth of `prototypes/shared/` without review
- New top-level `runtime/` or `platform/`
- pytest becoming required

---

## Escalate to lead when

Teammate proposes multi-week "framework sprint" from prototypes.
