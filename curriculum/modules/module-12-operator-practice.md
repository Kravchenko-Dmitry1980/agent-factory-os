# Module 12 — Operator Practice

## Goal

Use operator playbooks for daily work: run, troubleshoot, check, change safely.

## Simple Explanation

**Operator** = person who runs the lab, reads traces, evaluates behavior, and knows what not to touch — not DevOps for production.

## Key Ideas

- Runbooks with exact commands
- Troubleshooting without blind patching
- Checklists before/after change
- Role-based learning paths in operator-playbooks
