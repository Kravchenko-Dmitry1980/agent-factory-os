# How to Stop Hype Thinking

## Hype phrases to challenge

| Hype | Reality check question |
|------|------------------------|
| "Fully autonomous" | Who approves external actions? |
| "Self-improving" | Where is verification before writeback? |
| "Swarm of experts" | Where are contracts and escalation? |
| "Human in the loop optional" | Show deny path when human absent |
| "Production-ready demo" | Show deployment platform in repo? (No) |

## Classroom rule

If claim cannot point to **trace event** — mark as story, not architecture.

## Exercise

Students rewrite one hype sentence into a gated workflow sentence.

Example:  
Hype: "Agent publishes blog posts autonomously."  
Safe: "Agent drafts; human approves; then publish gate runs."
