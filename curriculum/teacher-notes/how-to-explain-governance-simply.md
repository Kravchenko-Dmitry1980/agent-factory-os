# How to Explain Governance Simply

> **Governance** = rules for what we allow into our official knowledge and our automated actions.

Two layers in this repo:

1. **Knowledge governance** — what enters `agent-os/` (promotion, provenance)
2. **Runtime governance** — gates in demos (approve, verify, fail-closed)

## Analogy

City building code — not bureaucracy for fun; prevents collapse when you add floors.

## Key phrase

"Governance before autonomy" — speed comes **after** gates exist.
