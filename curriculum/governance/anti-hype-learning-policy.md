# Anti-Hype Learning Policy

## Forbidden teaching claims

- "Fully autonomous agents"
- "AGI-ready architecture"
- "Replace human workers"
- "Production platform in this repo"
- "Model X makes verification unnecessary"
- "Swarm intelligence without gates"

## Required counter-narratives

- Verification-first
- Human approval for impact
- critic != truth; LLM != truth
- Small demos, not magic

## Classroom enforcement

Redirect hype sentences to gated workflow rewrite exercise.

Teacher notes: [../teacher-notes/how-to-stop-hype-thinking.md](../teacher-notes/how-to-stop-hype-thinking.md)

## Assessment

Students repeating hype without gate mapping fail explain-in-your-own-words sections.
