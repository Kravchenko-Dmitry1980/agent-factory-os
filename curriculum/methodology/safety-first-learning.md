# Safety-First Learning

## Order of values

1. **Safe** (gates work)
2. **Understandable** (trace explains)
3. **Correct** (content true — human judgment)
4. **Fast** (only after 1–3)

## Curriculum enforces

- Fail-path demos mandatory
- Approval and fail-closed before orchestration patterns
- Evaluation before "optimization" narratives

## Classroom mantra

"Fast wrong" is not a win.

## Assessment

Students who prioritize speed over gates fail practical sections even if articulate.
