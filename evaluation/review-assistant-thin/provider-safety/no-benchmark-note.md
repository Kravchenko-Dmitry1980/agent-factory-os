# No Benchmark Policy — Phase 3.4 Provider Safety

This harness is **not** a benchmark platform.

---

## Explicitly excluded

- No scores
- No ranking
- No leaderboard
- No model comparison
- No provider quality metrics
- No pass-rate trending as product KPI

---

## What we check instead

Whether unsafe synthetic provider-like output still results in safe system decisions (BLOCKED / ESCALATED / FAILED), not whether one model is "better" than another.
